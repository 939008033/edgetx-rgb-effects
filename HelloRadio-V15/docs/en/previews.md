# All 32 Animated Previews

[简体中文](../zh-CN/previews.md) · [Effect catalog](effects.md)

[Open all 32 effects in one GIF](../../previews/en/overview.gif)

One overview and 32 individual GIFs, each 12 seconds at 20 FPS and 65% preview brightness. B presets demonstrate throttle 0% → 100% → 0%; C04 demonstrates all three combos. B01 uses a rainbow palette, split expansion and inner-bottom start.

Frames come from real Lua RGB output with mocked hardware, not a handset recording. Diffusers, firmware and GIF quantization affect appearance. Each preview restarts after 12 seconds; not every effect ends its cycle exactly at that boundary.
